Enigma Development Studio is an independent, passionate, and ever-growing tech team that started its journey back in 2016.
It all began in high school, when Daniel Salehi and Reza Mahdipour decided to turn their curiosity into action — and build a team that breathes technology. Since then, we’ve been learning, growing, and pushing boundaries across various tech domains.

We believe the world of computers is full of elegant mysteries — and we love to decode them. The name Enigma reflects our passion for solving complex problems with creativity and code.
